How it works

Three things. Done quietly.

01

Archive

Tabs you stop using move to your Vault after 24 hours. Your browser stays clean, your research stays kept.

02

Smart Groups

Your archived tabs are quietly sorted by topic — so a whole study session comes back in a single click.

03

Restore

Caddy Vault reads your Google Calendar and has the right tabs waiting a few minutes before you sit down.

Why it exists

Every other tab tool lets you store tabs. Caddy Vault is the only one that brings them back, in the right shape, exactly when you need them.
