Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

The short version

Caddy Vault runs entirely inside your browser. We do not operate a server. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data with anyone. Your archived tabs, your search history, and your Google Calendar data never leave your machine, except for direct calls from your browser to Google's own APIs.

What the extension stores locally

Caddy Vault uses chrome.storage.local to save the following on your device:

  • Titles, URLs, and favicons of tabs you stop using (your "vault")
  • Topic groupings derived from those tabs
  • Reminder schedules generated from your calendar events
  • Your settings and preferences

All of this lives on your computer. Uninstalling the extension removes it.

Google Calendar access

If you connect Google Calendar (a Premium feature), Caddy Vault requests the calendar.readonly OAuth scope. We use this to read your upcoming event titles and times so the extension can surface the right tabs before each event. Your calendar data is never sent to us or to any third party. We never write to, modify, or delete events on your calendar.

You can revoke this access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Caddy Vault's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or telemetry inside the extension
  • No advertising, no tracking pixels, no third-party SDKs
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of any user data
  • No training of AI models on your data

This website

caddyvault.com is hosted on Vercel and uses Vercel's privacy-friendly aggregate analytics (page views, referrers). It does not set tracking cookies or identify individual visitors.

Payments

Premium purchases are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. Stripe's privacy policy is at stripe.com/privacy.

Children

Caddy Vault is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Changes

If this policy ever changes materially, we'll update the date at the top and note what changed.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@caddyvault.com.
